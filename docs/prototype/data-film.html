The problem

Facilities pay for energy waste they cannot see.

Building, plant and metering systems each keep their own data. Nothing joins them.

What we do · 1 Unify

One layer that turns scattered data into decisions.

It sits on top of the systems you already have. Nothing is replaced.

What we do · 2 Benchmark

Models learn what each machine should be using.

A neural network bound by the laws of physics predicts what each machine should use under today's conditions.

What we do · 3 Detect

The gap is the waste.

Anything above its benchmark is flagged, with the likely cause.

What we do · 4 Quantify

We don't rank alarms. We rank money.

Each inefficiency becomes a cost in OMR, ranked so you know what to fix first.
